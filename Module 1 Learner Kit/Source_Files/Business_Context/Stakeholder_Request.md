# SunnyVerse Renewable Group — Stakeholder Request

## Chief Operating Officer

> I want one view that tells me whether the renewable portfolio is performing, which regions are behind expectation, and where operations should investigate. I do not want teams arguing about whose spreadsheet is right.

## Head of Forecasting

> Actual versus forecast is important, but please do not call forecast a target. A forecast is our expected production based on available information; a target is a business objective.

## Finance Business Partner

> I need target attainment and portfolio context, but technical nameplate capacity should come from Operations & Engineering. Our asset register serves a different purpose and may contain values used for financial treatment.

## Storage Operations Lead

> Battery Hub West is storage. Do not add its 100 MW power rating to generation nameplate capacity and do not pretend state of charge is generation. The hybrid sites contain both generation and storage components.

## Data Governance Lead

> Every KPI needs an owner, definition, unit, denominator where relevant, and named source authority. If two sources disagree, the report must not silently choose one.

## Regional Operations Manager

> If a poor-generation day overlaps with maintenance, that is a clue, not proof that maintenance caused the shortfall. Weather, curtailment, equipment availability and other conditions may also matter.
