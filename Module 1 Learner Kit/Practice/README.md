# Practice

`03_Module_01_Practice_Workbook.xlsx` is your working workbook for Module 1.

Use it to record requirements, metric definitions, field classifications, quality findings, KPI design decisions, glossary entries, data-dictionary entries, reasoning exercises, checkpoint status, and validation notes.

It is outside `Source_Files/` because it is **your work product**, not an input dataset. Do not cite the workbook itself as source authority; cite the underlying supplied business or data source that supports your conclusion.

After you complete the workbook and reach the reference-comparison step in the Detailed Build Guide, you may compare your work with `../Reference/03_Module_01_Practice_Workbook_COMPLETED.xlsx`.
